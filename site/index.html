From report to reviewed action

  1. 01

    Bring your reports

    Paste report text or usernames and attach screenshots. DiscordChatExporter JSON imports are available under Advanced.

  2. 02

    Check each identity

    Open each report, compare its evidence and resolve the exact account. Approve and select only the users you intend to ban.

  3. 03

    Confirm your channel

    Review the prepared list and type the channel and count. Action history records results and lets you check uncertain outcomes or undo recorded bans.

Your Twitch account. Your review.

Both versions connect directly to Twitch with your permission. No shared report database, Discord account access or automatic AI verdicts. You need moderation permission in the target channel.
